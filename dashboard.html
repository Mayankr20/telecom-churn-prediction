ML Phase 1 • Exploratory Data Analysis

Key Churn Patterns & Business Hypotheses

Artifact: eda_insights.png

1. Month-to-Month Contract Vulnerability

Month-to-month contracts exhibit churn rates exceeding 42%, whereas customers on 1-year contracts drop to 11% and 2-year contracts to less than 3%. Securing an annual commitment is the highest leverage retention action.

2. Satisfaction Score Cliff (≤ 5 / 10)

Customers with satisfaction scores between 1 and 4 have churn probabilities ranging from 70% to 95%. A score of 5 still exhibits 45% churn. Immediate executive callbacks are mandated when satisfaction drops.

3. Billing Spike Trigger (15%+ Jump)

Customers experiencing a sudden billing jump (>15%) churn at over 51%, compared to ~16% for stable bills. This bill shock immediately primes them for competitor offers.

4. Network Degradation (Dropped Calls Doubled)

Churned customers report nearly double the average dropped calls (1.92 vs 1.02 in 30 days). Technical service resolution must accompany any commercial retention offer.

ML Phase 3 • Explainable AI (XAI)

Global SHAP Beeswarm Plot

Artifact: shap_summary.png

How to Read the Beeswarm:

Each dot represents an individual customer. Dots on the right (+ x-axis) push the prediction towards churn; dots on the left (- x-axis) pull toward retention. Red colors indicate high feature values, blue indicates low values.

1. SatisfactionScore (Top Impact): Blue dots (low satisfaction) spread far to the right (+0.15 SHAP), proving low satisfaction is the #1 churn driver across the entire company.
2. Tenure: Blue dots (short tenure ≤ 9 months) heavily bias toward churn (+0.12 SHAP). Loyal customers (>3 years) remain safe.
3. Contract_Two year: Red dots (having a 2-year contract) cluster on the extreme left (-0.20 SHAP), virtually eliminating churn risk.
4. InternetService_Fiber optic: High-speed fiber customers without dedicated tech support exhibit elevated churn due to network expectation mismatches.
5. BillingSpikePct & Flag: Sharp billing jumps push SHAP values right, triggering price-sensitivity churn.
